We're a student club at YK Pao School. We run charity sales and events, and use the money to pay for heart surgery for children whose families can't afford it.

Donate Meet the children

Started by students in 2024

Heart to Heart began as a student-led initiative at YK Pao School, driven by the belief that every child deserves a chance at a healthy life. Through fundraising events and community partnerships, we have united our school around a common cause: saving young lives.

A typical surgery costs around ¥30,000. We raise it through bake sales, drinks stalls, the Run For Hope charity run and donations from families, then pay the hospital directly for a specific child.

Newest project

The Sound of Hope

An immersive exhibition that transformed the school treehouse into a space for health education and emotional reflection. Through real heartbeat recordings of children who have recovered from congenital heart disease (CHD), patient stories, and visual displays, the project raises awareness of CHD and encourages greater empathy.
